- ranking=2: emphasizes winner-versus-loser ordering.
  - listwise=2: strong enough to optimize the whole race without dominating the loss.
  - cardinality=0.3: encourages approximately three selections without sacrificing ranking.
  - residual-scale=1: gives RaceFormer freedom to overturn the market.
  - residual-weight=0: does not penalize disagreement with the market.
  - learning-rate=0.00005: appropriate for the sensitive market-residual formulation.
  - patience=10: allows early noise but stops sustained validation deterioration.
